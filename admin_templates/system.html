Move a restaurant between computers

Export: open a restaurant → menu → “Copy, export & numbers” → Export. You get one zip with its website, photos and AI knowledge (no customer messages). Import that zip here, for example to bring a restaurant from your laptop to the server.

The imported restaurant is not live until you publish it. Its domain, Navira slug and channel IDs are left empty so it cannot answer for another restaurant.
