NAVIRA HUB - websites + AI chat + bookings for all your restaurants, in one place
==================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  One app with a dashboard (/admin). For every restaurant you can:
    - build and edit its website (type in forms, or just tell the AI what to change)
    - upload a menu photo or PDF and let the AI build the menu
    - upload photos, see a live preview, then press Publish
    - undo any change (Go live -> History)
    - turn the AI assistant on or off for Website chat, Facebook and Instagram
    - take table bookings in the chat through the Navira booking API
  The website and the chat use the same information, so you change things once.


=====================================================================
PART 1 - RUN IT ON YOUR LAPTOP (Windows)
=====================================================================
  1. Unzip navira-hub.zip. Open the navira-hub folder.
     (You should see app.py straight away, not another folder inside.)
  2. Click the folder's address bar, type  cmd  and press Enter.
  3. Type these, pressing Enter after each one:

        pip install -r requirements.txt
        set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=your-key
        set ADMIN_PASSWORD=choose-a-password
        python app.py

  4. Open your browser at:   http://127.0.0.1:5000/admin
     Log in with the password you chose.
  5. You will see Namaste Wolverhampton as an example. Click Edit.

  Keep the black window open while you use it. Close it to stop.


=====================================================================
PART 2 - HOW TO USE IT (2 minutes)
=====================================================================
  ADD A RESTAURANT
    Dashboard -> "+ Add restaurant" -> type the name -> Create.
    (Delete one: open it -> Go live -> Delete restaurant. It is moved to a
    "trash" folder on the server, not destroyed.)

  BUILD A WEBSITE JUST BY CHATTING  ("Build with AI" tab)
    The AI asks you questions and builds the website as you answer.
    1. Give it a head start, any of these:
         - paste the link to the restaurant's old website or Facebook page
         - click "Add photos or files" and attach the menu (photo or PDF),
           the logo and food photos (up to 12 files at once)
         - or just type: "Nepalese restaurant, 480 Dudley Road, 01902 939380,
           open 3pm to 10pm every day"
    2. It fills in what it can, shows a green list of what it did, and asks
       for the next missing thing (hours, links, booking, offers...).
       Answer, or type "skip".
    3. Watch the preview on the right change as you go.
    4. When you are happy, press "Publish website". It is live at /s/<short-name>/

  CHANGE SOMETHING LATER
    Open the restaurant and type it in the same chat, for example:
        Change Sunday hours to 12pm-9pm
        Chicken momo is now 11.50
        Remove the Christmas offer and add: 2 for 1 cocktails on Fridays
        Use the new photo as the main photo        (attach the photo)
    Check the preview, then press Publish.
    Nothing reaches customers until you press Publish.
    Wrong? Go live -> History -> "Go back to before this".
    Prefer typing in boxes? The other tabs (Basics, Menu, Photos...) edit the
    same website by hand.

  FESTIVE SECTION  ("Festive" tab, or ask the AI)
    A special coloured section near the top of the website for any festival or
    seasonal offer: Christmas menu, New Year's Eve, Valentine's, Mother's Day,
    Easter, Eid, Dashain, Tihar/Diwali, Halloween, or "Other" for anything else.
      - Pick the festival (it sets the colours), type a title, text and when.
      - Add set menus or deals with prices (optional) and a photo (optional).
      - Optional start / stop dates: it appears and disappears by itself.
      - Switch "Show the festive section" on, Save, check the preview, Publish.
    Or just tell the AI:
        Add a Dashain section: family feast 25 pounds per person, 10 to 24 October
        Take the Christmas section down
    While it is showing, the chat assistant also knows about it.
    One festive section at a time: change it when the season changes.

  DESIGN IT YOUR WAY  ("Design" tab, or just tell the AI)
    10 TEMPLATES: Elegant, Modern, Warm, Dark & bold, Minimal, Street food,
    Luxe, Fresh, Classic pub, Bistro. Click one in the Design tab. Your words,
    menu and photos stay; fonts, background and layouts change.
    "Use this template's colours" also takes its colours.

    A LAYOUT FOR EACH SECTION: top of the page, menu bar, offers, about,
    feature boxes, food menu, gallery, reviews, questions, hours & map. Each
    has its own choices (e.g. gallery: even grid / mixed sizes / sideways strip).

    MOVEMENT: none, gentle, or lively. Sections fade in as people scroll;
    lively adds a slow zoom on the top photo and lifting cards.

    OWN PAGES: in "Sections", tick "Own page" next to Menu, Gallery or any
    other section. It moves to its own page (/menu/, /gallery/) and the menu
    bar links to it. Untick to put it back on the home page.

    ALSO: colours, 22 fonts, button shape, corner roundness, section order,
    show/hide, your own headings, your own extra sections, custom CSS.

    OR TELL THE AI in "Build with AI", in your own words:
        Use the Luxe template with our gold colour
        Put the menu and gallery on their own pages
        Show the gallery as a sideways strip and the reviews as big quotes
        Make it move more / turn the animations off
        Put the menu first, and hide the reviews
        Add a section about our rooms with this photo        (attach the photo)
    Check Desktop AND Mobile in the preview, then Publish.

    ON PHONES every website has a menu button (three lines) at the top right,
    plus Call / Order / Book buttons at the bottom.

  THE ADMIN MENU  (the three-line button, top left of a restaurant)
    Every settings screen is in this one list: Name & texts, Opening hours,
    Links, Offers, Festive, Feature boxes, Food menu, Photos & gallery,
    Reviews, Questions, Your own sections, Template & layouts, Section order
    & pages, Table booking, Chat assistant, Facebook & Instagram, AI
    knowledge, Messages & bookings, Website address, Privacy, History, Delete.
    On a phone or small screen, tap the button to open the list.

  OFFERS WITH A PHOTO, BUTTON AND LINK  (menu -> Offers)
    Each offer can have a photo, a button text and a link. Choose where the
    photo goes: above the text, under the text, or behind the text. The same
    choice is on feature boxes, cards in your own sections, and the festive
    section. A button with no link opens the table booking form.
    The chip at the top of the page shows each offer in turn, every 5 seconds.

  TABLE BOOKING ON THE WEBSITE (runs on Navira)
    Chat & channels tab -> "Navira booking slug" (use  naviratest  to test).
    Every "Book a table" button then opens the website's own booking form:
    1 date (month calendar) -> 2 how many people -> 3 free times -> your
    details -> check -> booked. The last screen shows the reference and ALL
    the booking details, with "Add to calendar" and "Print".
    The booking goes straight into your Navira system and Navira sends the
    confirmation email. There is also a page to share:  /s/<short-name>/book/
    No slug? The buttons open the "Table booking link" instead.
    FOUR LOOKS for the form (menu -> Table booking -> Booking form style):
    step by step in the middle, a panel sliding in from the right, full
    screen with a summary at the side, or a quick form on one screen.
    BIGGEST GROUP: set "Biggest group that can book online". When a customer
    picks that number, the form says: for a bigger group, please contact the
    restaurant directly, with the phone number.
    TEST IT: make one booking with your own email, check it is in Navira,
    then cancel it there.

  MESSAGES & CHANNELS  (dashboard -> "Messages")
    One place for the website chat, Facebook and Instagram, for all restaurants.
      Conversations: read what customers and the AI said. "Take over from AI"
          pauses the AI for that customer; type a reply and Send; "Hand back
          to AI" when you are done. Replies go to Facebook/Instagram straight
          away, and into the website chat window if the visitor still has it open.
      Bookings: a list of bookings made by the website form and by the AI.
          (Changes and cancellations are done in Navira.)
      Channels: AI on/off for every restaurant and channel, and what still
          needs setting up.
    Conversations are kept for 30 days, then deleted automatically.

  PRIVACY NOTICE  (Go live tab)
    The booking form and chat collect names, emails and phone numbers, so each
    website should have a privacy page. Click "Write a starting text for me",
    check it, Save, Publish. It appears as "Privacy" at the bottom of the site.
    Meta asks for this page too before it approves Facebook/Instagram messaging.

  WHAT THE CHAT ASSISTANT ANSWERS FROM  ("AI knowledge" tab)
    The customer chat (website, Facebook, Instagram) knows:
       - everything on the PUBLISHED website (menu, hours, offers...)
       - plus everything you add in "AI knowledge":
            upload files or photos (allergen sheet, party packages, drinks list,
            a photo of the specials board), add a web page link, or type a note.
    These are used straight away, no Publish needed. Delete one and the
    assistant stops using it.

  ALWAYS CHECK what the AI read from a menu photo (prices!) before you publish.

  BOOKINGS IN THE CHAT
    Chat & channels tab -> "Navira booking slug" -> the restaurant's Navira slug
    (use  naviratest  for testing). Save.

  AI ON / OFF
    Dashboard switches, or Chat & channels tab. Works straight away.


=====================================================================
PART 3 - MAKE IT LIVE ON THE INTERNET (Render)
=====================================================================
  A. PUT THE CODE ON GITHUB
     1. github.com -> + -> New repository -> name: navira-hub -> Private -> Create.
        (Do NOT add a README.)
     2. In the black window, inside the navira-hub folder:

          git init
          git add .
          git commit -m "navira hub"
          git branch -M main
          git remote add origin https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/navira-hub.git
          git push -u origin main

  B. CREATE THE SERVER
     1. render.com -> New + -> Web Service -> pick navira-hub.
     2. Fill in:
          Language:        Python 3
          Build Command:   pip install -r requirements.txt
          Start Command:   gunicorn app:app --workers 1 --threads 8 --timeout 180
     3. Environment Variables -> add:
          ANTHROPIC_API_KEY = your key
          ADMIN_PASSWORD    = a strong password
          SECRET_KEY        = any long random text (keeps you logged in)
     4. Deploy. When it says "Live", open:
          https://YOUR-RENDER-ADDRESS/admin

  C. KEEP YOUR WORK SAFE  (VERY IMPORTANT)
     On Render's FREE plan the server's files are wiped every time it restarts
     or you push new code. That would delete the websites and photos you made
     in the dashboard. Before you use it for real restaurants:
       1. Change the service to a paid instance type (see Render's pricing page).
       2. In the service -> Disks -> Add Disk:
             Mount Path:  /var/data        Size: 1 GB is plenty to start
       3. Environment Variables -> add:   DATA_DIR = /var/data
       4. Deploy again.
     Now everything you do in the dashboard is kept.
     (A paid instance also does not "sleep", so the chat answers at once.)

  D. EACH RESTAURANT'S WEBSITE ADDRESS
     Straight away:   https://YOUR-RENDER-ADDRESS/s/namaste/
     Their own domain (e.g. www.namastewolverhampton.co.uk):
       1. Render -> the service -> Settings -> Custom Domains -> Add the domain.
       2. Where the domain was bought (GoDaddy, Namecheap...), add the DNS
          record Render shows you. Wait until Render says "Verified".
       3. Dashboard -> the restaurant -> Go live -> type the domain -> Save.
     The domain now shows that restaurant's website, with https.

  E. UPDATING THE APP LATER (new code from me)
       git add .
       git commit -m "update"
       git push
     Render redeploys by itself. Your restaurants stay (they live on the Disk).


=====================================================================
PART 4 - FACEBOOK & INSTAGRAM (optional)
=====================================================================
  The same AI answers messages sent to the restaurant's Facebook Page and
  Instagram account, and books tables.

  YOU NEED
    - Admin access to the restaurant's Facebook Page.
    - For Instagram: a Business/Professional account linked to that Page, with
      "Allow access to messages" turned on in the Instagram app.
    - A free account at developers.facebook.com

  ONE TIME (Meta's screens change often; names may differ a little)
    1. developers.facebook.com -> My Apps -> Create App (type: Business).
    2. Add the "Messenger" product (and Instagram messaging inside it).
    3. App settings -> Basic -> copy the "App secret".
    4. Render -> Environment Variables:
          META_APP_SECRET   = the App secret
          META_VERIFY_TOKEN = any password you make up
    5. Meta app -> Messenger settings -> Webhooks:
          Callback URL:  https://YOUR-RENDER-ADDRESS/webhooks/meta
          Verify token:  the same META_VERIFY_TOKEN
       Tick the "messages" field for the Page and for Instagram.

  FOR EACH RESTAURANT
    1. In the Meta app: connect the restaurant's Page -> Generate token.
    2. Render -> Environment Variables: add the token with the name shown in the
       dashboard (Chat & channels tab), e.g.  PAGE_TOKEN_NAMASTE
    3. Dashboard -> restaurant -> Chat & channels: type the Facebook Page ID and
       Instagram account ID -> Save.
       Don't know the ID? Send the Page a message, then look at Render -> Logs
       for a line starting with [META]; it tells you the ID.
    4. Message the Page from your own account: "table for 2 tomorrow?"

  BEFORE REAL CUSTOMERS CAN USE IT
    Until Meta approves your app, the AI only answers people who have a role on
    the Meta app (you and testers). For the public you need Business
    Verification and App Review ("Advanced access") for pages_messaging and
    instagram_manage_messages, plus a privacy policy page. Allow days to weeks.

  Never put tokens or secrets in the code or on GitHub. Only in Render's
  Environment Variables.


=====================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
=====================================================================
  Render -> Logs shows what happened:
    [API]      calls to the Navira booking system
    [BOOKED]   a booking was made
    [AI EDIT]  the AI changed a website
    [META]     Facebook / Instagram
    [ERROR]    something failed (send me this line)

  "The AI is not connected"  -> ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is missing or wrong.
  Can't log in               -> ADMIN_PASSWORD is not set, or you typed it wrong.
  My restaurants disappeared -> see Part 3 C (add the Disk and DATA_DIR).
  Booking form says it can't check tables -> look for [API] lines in the logs;
                                the Navira slug may be wrong or booking is off in Navira.

FESTIVALS (colours for the festive section)
  Admin > Festivals (button at the top of the Restaurants page).
  One list for all restaurants. Change a festival's colours, add your own festival
  (name + 4 colours), or remove one. Then open a restaurant > Festive section and
  choose it under "Festival (sets the colours)".
  The list is saved in the data folder as festivals.json.

BACKUPS  (Admin > Backups & tools)
  A backup of the whole data folder is made once a day and kept in <DATA_DIR>/backups
  (the newest 7; change with BACKUP_KEEP in the env file, switch off with AUTO_BACKUP=0).
  "Back up now" makes one straight away. "Download" saves it to your computer.
  Download one every week or so: a backup on the same server is lost if the server is lost.
  To put a backup back: systemctl stop navira-hub, unzip it into the data folder,
  systemctl start navira-hub.

NUMBERS
  Each restaurant card shows visitors, chat messages and bookings for the last 30 days.
  Day by day: open a restaurant > menu > "Copy, export & numbers". All restaurants together:
  Admin > Backups & tools. No cookies are used and nothing personal is stored.

COPY / EXPORT / IMPORT A RESTAURANT
  Copy:   "Copy" on a restaurant card. New restaurant with the same design, menu, photos.
  Export: open a restaurant > "Copy, export & numbers" > Export as zip.
  Import: Admin > Backups & tools > "Move a restaurant between computers".
  Use export + import to move a restaurant from your laptop to the server.
  A copy or import is not live until you publish it, and its Navira slug, domain and
  channel IDs are empty so it can never take bookings for the wrong restaurant.

AI MONTHLY LIMIT
  Open a restaurant > "AI monthly limit": the most AI replies in one month. When reached,
  the assistant tells customers to call instead, and starts again on the 1st.
  AI_MONTHLY_LIMIT in the env file sets one limit for every restaurant that has 0.

COOKIE NOTICE
  Open a restaurant > "Privacy & cookie notice". On by default, shown once per visitor.
  The website itself uses no tracking cookies. If you add Google Analytics, a Facebook
  pixel or similar with custom code, you need a proper consent tool instead.

WHATSAPP
  Uses the WhatsApp Business Cloud API from the same Meta app as Facebook and Instagram.
  1. In the Meta app add the "WhatsApp" product and add the restaurant's phone number.
     (A number used here cannot be used in the normal WhatsApp app at the same time.)
  2. Webhook: the same address as Facebook (/webhooks/meta) and the same verify token.
     Subscribe to the "messages" field.
  3. Make a permanent token (Business settings > System users) with whatsapp_business_messaging.
     Put it in the env file as WHATSAPP_TOKEN_<ID> (for example WHATSAPP_TOKEN_NAMASTE),
     or as one WHATSAPP_TOKEN if all numbers are in the same WhatsApp Business account.
  4. Open the restaurant > WhatsApp > paste the Phone number ID > Save.
  5. Chat assistant > switch WhatsApp on.
  Replies inside 24 hours of the customer's message are normal replies. Meta charges for
  some WhatsApp messages; check Meta's current prices before switching it on.

SECTION TEMPLATES (10 for each section)
  Open a restaurant > menu > "Section templates (10 each)". Offers, Festive, Feature boxes,
  Food menu, Reviews, Gallery, Hours/map/contact and Questions each have 10 looks.
  Reviews "One review at a time" and "Row of cards" slide by themselves.
  Gallery: tap a photo to open it big, then swipe or use the arrows for the next one.

MENU PHOTOS AND SECTION BUTTONS
  Food menu: each dish and each menu section can have a photo (upload in Photos first).
  "Section buttons": a row you slide sideways, or a ☰ button that opens the list.

ONLINE ORDERING AND SOCIAL LINKS
  "Online ordering & delivery apps": your own ordering link is always the main button.
  Just Eat, Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Foodhub are shown smaller as "Also on".
  "Social media links": Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, WhatsApp, Tripadvisor,
  Snapchat, Threads, LinkedIn.

MENU BAR, PHONE BAR, FOOTER CREDIT
  "Menu bar & phone bar": choose which sections are named in the top bar, which buttons it
  has, and up to 4 buttons for the bar at the bottom on phones (or no bar).
  "Footer & Navira credit": choose the Navira Technology line, write your own, or hide it.

SWITCH A WEBSITE OFF, OR DELETE A RESTAURANT
  Open a restaurant > menu > "Switch off or delete".
  Switch off: the website link shows "This website is not available right now". Nothing is
  deleted, and you can switch it on again any time.
  Delete: type the word delete in the box, then the Delete button works.

COUNTRY AND REGION
  "+ Add restaurant" asks which country and region the restaurant is in.
  The dashboard can be filtered by country, region, and live / not live.
  Change them later: open the restaurant > "Name & texts" > "Country and region".
